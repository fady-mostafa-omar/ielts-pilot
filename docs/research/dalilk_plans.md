## The 90-day plan video (owner's link, 2026-11-03)

"كيف تجتاز الايلتس من الصفر؟ خطة مكثفة في ٣ اشهر" — Dalilk 4 IELTS (Abdulrahman Hegazy),
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pUrZ5akZRao, published 2024-08-01, 20:34. Read from YouTube's
auto-generated Arabic captions (noisy; names of outside sources may be misheard). The description
links the same PDF (Google Drive) and recommends watching the development-hour video first
(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MMUB_Tq-FP0); the PDF footnote links https://youtu.be/_v8tUgcgLOc.

What the video says (paraphrased):

- 3 hours a day for 12 weeks = **2 focus hours + 1 development hour**, every day of the week.
- **Development hour, unchanged for all 12 weeks:** 30 min listening, 20 min reading, 5 min writing,
  5 min speaking. Material gets harder as the level rises; the minutes stay the same. Best kept at a
  fixed time of day, apart from the focus hours (e.g. focus in the morning or after work,
  development hour after dinner or before sleep).
- **Weeks 1–2 — focus = listening:** 2 h of podcasts at the learner's level (intermediate material
  for intermediate learners, harder English podcasts for higher levels). Listening is "the
  backbone". It can run during low-attention activities (exercise, walking, housework, commuting)
  but with attention; 5-min breaks every ~30 min are fine; the 2 h must be completed.
- **Weeks 3–4 — focus = reading novels** at the learner's level (harder/longer for advanced,
  simpler for intermediate); stories rather than IELTS passages because they are enjoyable input
  that also helps writing, grammar and vocabulary.
- **Week 5 — Listening (exam):** days 1–2 techniques (channel videos or Dalilk's free IELTS book),
  days 3–7 practice tests under exam conditions. Expect day 3 to go badly and days 5–7 to click.
- **Week 6 — Reading (exam):** days 1–2 techniques, days 3–7 practice tests (links in the PDF).
- **Week 7 — Writing:** Task 1 three days, Task 2 four days; first day techniques, then writing.
  He advises sending each answer to an online corrector or AI for feedback (spelling, vocabulary,
  ideas).
- **Week 8 — Speaking:** day 1 techniques (~2 h is enough), then the expected questions, which he
  says are updated about every four months.
- **Weeks 9–12:** month 2 again (listening, reading, writing, speaking) as consolidation —
  techniques become review, more practice. Full practice tests (~3.5 h) in week 12 and on the last
  two days before the exam.
- His claim that the plan takes someone from zero to band 7 is his own; no evidence is given.

Compared with planner-3 (CURRICULUM §4a): same development split (30/20/5/5 ≈ D-019's
30/20/10). Different shape: Dalilk rotates a **2-hour weekly focus** (listening → reading → exam
skills one per week, twice) on top of a **fixed 1-hour development hour**; planner-3 mixes every
skill every day by phase share and only rotates the exam skill weekly. Dalilk has no full mock
before week 12; D-024 (owner) adds one every two weeks. Dalilk's final: 2–3 days of full tests;
D-024: 3 days of full tests + 1 day techniques review.
