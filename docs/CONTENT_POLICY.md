# IELTS Pilot — Content Policy (personal edition)

Status: **draft for approval** · 2026-11-02

---

1. **Scope.** IELTS Pilot is a personal, non-distributed study tool (D-001). If that ever changes, this policy must be rewritten **before** any distribution, and the master spec §2 content-rights rules apply in full.

2. **Third-party files** (Dalilk PDFs, official practice tests, books) are downloaded by the owner from where the provider offers them. They are stored only under `%APPDATA%\IELTS Pilot\library\`. They are never committed to the repository, never included in the installer, and never uploaded anywhere.

3. **The repository holds metadata only:** titles, URLs, provider, licence notes, and our own mapping to activity types.

4. **No scraping and no bypassing** of logins, paywalls, forms or technical restrictions. Online tools open as live pages in the in-app web panel. Exception approved by the owner (D-030, D-041): public feeds and two public Dalilk pages (the 350 words, the 150 phrases) are read on the owner's own computer for his personal study, the way a browser reads them, and kept only in the local database — nothing is redistributed, no login is involved.

5. **Every resource records** provider, URL and a licence note. Dalilk's note: "Free access. No licence or terms of use found on the library page (checked 2026-11-02). Personal use only."

6. **Official IELTS material** is used for personal practice only. Official band descriptors are linked, not embedded. The app's checklists use our own wording built on the four official criteria (D-012).

7. **"Expected questions" resources** (e.g. Dalilk's expected speaking questions, DLK-19) may be based on questions recalled from live tests. They are used as practice prompts only and never presented as official IELTS material.

8. **Original content** (prompts, checklists, later exercises) is labelled `provider = Original`. Content drafted with AI help is labelled as such and reviewed by the owner before use.

9. **Essays and recordings are private data.** They stay on the machine. Any future online feature (e.g. AI feedback) requires the owner's explicit consent each time content leaves the machine.

10. **Name.** "IELTS Pilot" is a private working name. IELTS is a trademark of the IELTS partners; no affiliation is implied.

11. **Dalilk inventory coverage.** `content/catalog/dalilk_inventory.csv` lists the Foundation, Fluency and IELTS tracks of the library as seen on 2026-11-02. The STEP and TOEFL tracks are excluded as out of scope. Only the library page was checked; individual resources have not been opened yet, so their content, Academic/General focus and access method are marked unverified where relevant.
